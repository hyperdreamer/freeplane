What happens to the graph view on F11?

Your viewport is a center point plus a zoom factor. Entering full screen gives the canvas much more room — the question is whether the graph keeps your exact view or re-fits. (The dashed outline marks where the window used to be.)

A — Keep your exact view recommended

Same center and same zoom; extra space reveals more of the graph around your focus. Exiting returns precisely to the previous view. Nothing moves unexpectedly.

dashed box = old window area · new surroundings appear at the edges

B — Fit graph on entry, restore view on exit

F11 re-fits the whole graph to the screen (zoom changes). Exiting returns to your previous viewport, so the fitted view is temporary.

entry always shows everything · exit = exact previous view

C — Fit graph on entry and keep it

F11 re-fits and the fitted view stays after exit — the windowed view comes back showing the whole graph. Your previous zoom is gone.

entry and exit both show the whole graph

Current actions (zoom, fit, pan, node drag) work normally in fullscreen in all three options. Click a card to select, then confirm in the terminal.
